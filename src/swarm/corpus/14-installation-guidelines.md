# Installation Guidelines for Modular Heat Pump Skids

These guidelines summarise the commissioning requirements that a certified installation partner must satisfy for the warranty to apply. The full manual ships with every unit and in the partner portal.

Siting. Place the outdoor skid on a level concrete or vibration-damped frame with the acoustic side facing away from bedrooms and neighbouring boundaries. Maintain 300 millimetres of clearance on the acoustic side and at least 600 millimetres on the service side. Because the compressor module is the dominant sound source, a 2.5 metre distance from reflective facades keeps field sound pressure close to the declared figures.

Hydraulics. Install the flow filter upstream of the hydronic module and fill the system with treated water meeting the water-quality table in the manual. The isolation valves must allow the heat exchanger module to be exchanged without draining the whole system. Minimum system volume requirements apply for stability at low modulation; the commissioning app computes this from the radiator or underfloor surface data.

Electrics and controls. Connect the grid supply through the provided isolator with type-A RCD protection. The control module supports OpenTherm and the local API; commissioning requires the partner account token issued after training. After power-up, run the automatic refrigerant-charge verification, which compares subcooling against the reference curve for the installed compressor revision.

Handover. Record commissioning in the portal within four weeks, attach the water-quality certificate, and brief the owner on the annual visual inspection. The commissioning app will not issue the final registration QR code until the acoustic self-test and charge verification have both passed, which is deliberate: unregistered units cannot be enrolled in telemetry-based warranty diagnostics.

# Certified Installer Partner Network

Kaltwerk sells exclusively through a certified partner network; there is no direct-to-consumer installation channel. This document describes the network's structure, requirements, and current scale.

Scale and coverage. At the end of 2024 the network counted more than 500 certified installation partners across twelve European countries, meeting the expansion target set in the 2023 capacity review. Partner density is highest in southern Germany, Austria, and the Netherlands, with dedicated onboarding tracks for the Nordics where propane handling certification requirements differ.

Certification. Certification requires a three-day training covering hydronic design for modulating heat pumps, R-290 handling, commissioning with the app, and warranty diagnostics. Recertification happens every three years, and partners who miss two commissioning-quality audits are suspended until retrained. The partner portal tracks each technician's certificates individually, not just per company.

Obligations and benefits. Certified partners commit to the commissioning checklist, the maintenance log, and 48-hour spare-module dispatch handling. In return they receive protected leads from the manufacturer's website, extended margins on service contracts, and direct access to the second-line engineering desk. The partner satisfaction survey from spring 2025 shows the highest satisfaction scores in the network's history, driven by the telemetry-based diagnostics that shorten fault-finding on site.

Quality loop. Commissioning data feeds back into product engineering: recurring hydraulic-instability findings triggered the minimum system volume guidance in the installation guidelines, and the installation app now blocks final registration until the acoustic self-test passes, closing the loop that previously let misconfigured units reach customers.

# HX-12 Plate Heat Exchanger Module: Design Notes

The HX-12 heat exchanger is the condenser module used across the K-Series product line. It is a brazed-plate unit with 120 plates, stainless steel 316 construction, and a nominal capacity of 11 kW at standard rating conditions. Design notes below summarise the engineering rationale for the 2025 revision.

Material choice. Nickel-brazed 316 plates were selected over aluminum because R-290 refrigerant circuits in the field showed pitting corrosion on aluminum microchannel coils in coastal installations. The HX-12 heat exchanger therefore carries a ten-year pressure-boundary warranty in the standard service manual, longer than any other module in the range.

Flow optimisation. The 2025 revision changed the port geometry to reduce pressure drop by roughly 12 percent at nominal flow, which recovers a small amount of compressor head and improves seasonal efficiency. The change required no resizing of the circulating pump in most system designs.

Service concept. The HX-12 heat exchanger is one of the three field-replaceable modules in the K-Series architecture. Replacement takes a certified partner about 90 minutes, and the hydronic module does not need to be drained below the isolation valve level. Inspection intervals in the maintenance schedule call for a visual leak check every two years and a full performance verification every six years.

Fouling behaviour was characterised on a rig running hard water at 30 degrees Celsius for 4,000 hours; fouling factor stayed within 4 percent of the clean value with a standard magnetic filter installed. The design team's conclusion is that with correct water treatment the HX-12 heat exchanger reaches its intended service life without chemical cleaning in the overwhelming majority of installations.

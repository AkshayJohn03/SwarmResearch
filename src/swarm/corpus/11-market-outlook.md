# European Heat Pump Market Outlook 2026

Market research summary prepared for planning purposes, October 2025. All figures are analyst estimates for the European Economic Area.

After two years of flat demand, the European heat pump market is expected to return to growth in 2026, with hydronic air-to-water unit sales forecast to rise between 6 and 11 percent depending on the subsidy environment in the three largest markets. Germany remains the largest single market, followed by France and Italy, with the Nordics stable in volume but shifting strongly toward propane and natural refrigerant units.

Drivers. Building-sector decarbonisation obligations, the phase-out schedules for fossil boilers in several national laws, and falling electricity-to-gas price ratios are the structural drivers. The analyst view is that subsidy design matters more than technology: markets with predictable, investment-tied subsidy schemes show consistently higher replacement rates than markets with stop-and-go schemes.

Supply side. Component suppliers report expanding inverter and compressor capacity through 2026, easing the shortages that constrained the industry in previous years. Refrigerant transition is a competitive differentiator: propane-based products are gaining share in installers' preference surveys because of the quota-free refrigerant supply and simpler end-of-life handling.

Risks to the forecast include a slower-than-expected grid connection queue for larger electrification projects, installer capacity bottlenecks in rural regions, and potential changes to electricity levies. Manufacturers with modular architectures and strong service networks are rated best positioned, because installation and maintenance capacity, not factory output, is the binding constraint in most national markets through the end of the decade.

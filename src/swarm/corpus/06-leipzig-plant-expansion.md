# Leipzig Plant Expansion Reaches Full Capacity

Leipzig, 5 September 2025. Kaltwerk announced that the second assembly hall at the Leipzig plant has reached full utilisation after a nine-month ramp. With both halls running two shifts, the Leipzig plant now ships 11,500 modular heat pump units per year, up from roughly 6,000 units annually before the expansion.

The expansion added a dedicated compressor sub-assembly line where the XK-7 compressor module is married to its isolation frame and inverter before being delivered just-in-time to the main line. Quality engineering introduced inline acoustic screening on this line: every compressor module is run through a 90-second sound test, and units whose acoustic signature deviates from the reference profile are diverted to a diagnostics cell rather than shipped.

Logistics around the Leipzig plant were reorganised at the same time. R-290 cylinders are now delivered in returnable racks, and the hydronic module supplier moved onto the same industrial park, cutting inbound transport for the heaviest components almost entirely. The plant holds a strategic buffer of two weeks of compressor modules to dampen supply shocks.

Workforce. The expansion created about 180 jobs, mostly in assembly and test engineering. Kaltwerk runs its own apprenticeship programme for mechatronics and reports a first-year retention above 85 percent.

Outlook. Management states that the current layout can reach roughly 14,000 units per year with added weekend shifts before a third hall would be required, and that a decision on a third hall is expected next year depending on subsidy cycles and export demand from the Nordics.

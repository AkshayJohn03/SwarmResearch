# Q3 2025 Financial Memo

Internal memo to the management board, 14 October 2025. Figures are unaudited and rounded; this memo is published internally only.

Revenue. Third-quarter revenue reached 61 million euros, up 24 percent year over year, driven by German subsidy-funded replacements and the first full quarter at full capacity after the plant expansion. Order intake exceeded shipments for the fourth consecutive quarter, and the order book now covers roughly five months of production.

Margins. Gross margin improved to 34 percent from 31 percent a year earlier. The improvement is attributable to three factors: the returnable packaging loop cutting inbound transport cost, the second compressor source removing the premium previously paid for expedited single-source freight, and lower warranty cost following the telemetry-based diagnostics rollout. Material inflation on copper and electronics remained broadly neutral in the quarter.

Cash and capex. Free cash flow was positive despite continued investment in the third assembly hall feasibility study. Capital expenditure focused on the inline acoustic screening extension and the magnet recycling pilot announced with the end-of-life programme. No changes to the financing structure are proposed.

Risks. The memo flags three watch items for the board: subsidy timing in the largest market, rare-earth allocation for rotor magnets described in the supply-chain risk register, and installer capacity in rural regions, which the market outlook identifies as the industry-wide growth constraint. Management reiterates the guidance issued at mid-year and does not recommend changing it on the basis of this memo alone.

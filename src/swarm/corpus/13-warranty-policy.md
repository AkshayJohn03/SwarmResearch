# Warranty and Service Policy

Effective 1 January 2025, this policy defines the warranty terms for K-Series modular heat pumps sold in the European Economic Area.

Coverage. Kaltwerk provides a seven-year warranty on all K-Series modules when the unit is commissioned by a certified partner and registered in the partner portal within four weeks of commissioning. The warranty covers parts and labour for defects in materials and workmanship under normal operating conditions. Compressor modules are covered under the same term; no separate compressor schedule exists.

Conditions. Coverage requires documented commissioning with the standard checklist, annual visual inspection by the owner or a partner, and refrigerant work performed only by partners holding the flammable-refrigerant certificate. Damage from freezing, flooding, unauthorised refrigerant charge, or operation outside the declared envelope is excluded. Using non-genuine replacement modules voids coverage of the affected subsystem.

Service response. Warranty service is delivered through the certified partner network with a target first-visit fix rate above 90 percent, supported by the telemetry portal and the per-module health ledger. Spare modules are stocked regionally with a promised dispatch within 48 hours for in-warranty claims. If a module fails three times within a warranty year, the policy entitles the owner to a full module substitution with the current revision.

Extended commercial terms. Light-commercial installations can purchase coverage extensions up to ten years at contract time. Transfer of warranty to a new owner is free of charge and processed through the portal. Claims history shows that the warranty programme's cost ratio has been stable, which management attributes to early fault isolation through factory acoustic fingerprints and firmware diagnostics rather than to conservative claim handling.

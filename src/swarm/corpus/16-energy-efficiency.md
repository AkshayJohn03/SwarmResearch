# Seasonal Efficiency Data: COP and SCOP

This document summarises certified efficiency figures for the K-Series and explains the operating conditions behind them. All figures are measured per EN 14511 for rating points and declared per EN 14825 for seasonal values, with certificates from the independent notified body.

Rating point. At the standard low-temperature rating point A-7/W35, the K-Series reaches a coefficient of performance of 4.6, meaning 4.6 units of heat per unit of electricity at seven degrees below zero outside and 35 degrees Celsius flow temperature. At the A2/W35 rating point the measured COP is 5.1, and at A-15/W45 the system still delivers a COP above 2.4, which is the figure relevant for cold-climate sizing without electrical backup.

Seasonal figures. For the reference climate stratum in the energy labelling package, the declared seasonal space-heating efficiency corresponds to an A+++ class label. Field telemetry from the connected fleet tracks within 3 percent of the declared seasonal value when systems are commissioned to the hydraulic stability rules, and within 7 percent when older radiators force higher flow temperatures.

Sensitivity. Efficiency is most sensitive to flow temperature: each 5 kelvin reduction in design flow temperature improves seasonal efficiency by roughly 8 percent in the fleet data. The model-predictive modulation introduced in the current firmware improves part-load behaviour further, which is why low-load buildings no longer show the cycling losses visible in earlier fleet generations.

Owners comparing products should insist on seasonal figures for the correct climate stratum and on the same W-temperature, because rating-point numbers quoted at different conditions are not comparable across manufacturers.

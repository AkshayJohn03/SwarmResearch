# XK-7 Compressor Module: Technical Specification Sheet

This specification sheet applies to the XK-7 compressor module, revision D, as shipped in K-Series heat pumps from March 2025 onwards. The XK-7 compressor module is a variable-speed rotary unit with an integrated inverter drive and a coated aluminum heat exchanger surface for corrosion protection.

Rated electrical input at nominal conditions is 2.9 kW with a modulation range of 15 to 110 percent. The refrigerant connection uses brazed fittings sized for R-290. The module weighs 34 kilograms including the isolation frame, and service clearances of 300 millimetres must be respected on the acoustic side.

Acoustic rating: the rated sound pressure level of the XK-7 compressor module is 47 dB(A) at three metres, measured in free-field conditions per the internal Kaltwerk measurement standard KWS-11. Installers should treat the 47 dB(A) rating of the XK-7 compressor module as the contractual figure when siting units near bedroom windows or property boundaries. The rating assumes the standard isolation frame; fitting the optional acoustic hood reduces the measured figure further, although Kaltwerk does not publish hood-adjusted values on this sheet.

Operating envelope for the XK-7 compressor module spans evaporating temperatures down to -25 degrees Celsius, which supports heating operation in Scandinavian climate zones without backup electrical resistance in most building standards. The inverter supports a 12 to 120 hertz modulation band.

Firmware compatibility requires KaltOS 4.1 or newer. Older control modules can be field-upgraded by a certified partner. Warranty coverage for the compressor follows the general product warranty terms and is voided if the module is run outside the declared envelope or with unapproved refrigerant charges.

# Production and Capacity Review 2023

Internal review, November 2023. This document is retained for historical context and reflects the situation at the end of 2023; several figures have since been superseded by newer planning documents.

At the time of writing, the Leipzig plant produced about 4,000 modular heat pump units per year on a single assembly hall and one shift pattern. The plant struggled through the summer with compressor deliveries after the drive-electronics shortage, and output in the third quarter fell roughly 18 percent below plan before recovering in October.

The compressor situation dominated the review. Kaltwerk was single-sourced on the previous compressor generation, and management committed to qualifying a second compressor site and moving to dual sourcing for the next generation. The review also notes that the control stack then in service, KaltOS 2.3, received only critical security updates because the firmware team was fully allocated to the platform rewrite.

Commercially, the review records a five-year warranty on the complete unit as the standard offering, with extended terms sold as a paid add-on for commercial customers. Installation partner capacity was flagged as the binding growth constraint: only 220 certified partners existed at the end of 2023, concentrated in southern Germany.

Recommendations adopted in the review were: dual-source the next compressor generation, expand the certified partner programme to at least 500 partners, and move refrigerant logistics to returnable racks. Readers should treat every quantity in this document as a 2023 snapshot and consult the current plant and product documentation for present-day values.

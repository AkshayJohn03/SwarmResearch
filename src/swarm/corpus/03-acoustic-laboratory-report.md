# Acoustic Laboratory Report: XK-7 Sound Pressure Measurements

Author: Kaltwerk Acoustics Laboratory, Leipzig. Test standard: EN 12102-1 adapted with internal procedure KWS-11-A. This report covers production revision C of the XK-7 compressor module installed in a standard K-Series outdoor cabinet.

Test setup. The module was mounted on the production isolation frame inside a semi-anechoic chamber with a background floor below 20 dB(A). Microphones were placed at three metres in four directions, at one metre height. Measurements were taken at nominal heating duty, at 50 percent modulation, and at maximum speed. Each configuration was measured three times and averaged per the standard.

Results. At nominal duty, the unit registered a sound pressure level of 42 dB(A) at three metres for the XK-7 compressor module. At 50 percent modulation the reading fell to 36 dB(A), and at maximum speed it rose to 51 dB(A). The 42 dB(A) nominal figure is the value quoted in customer documentation for the XK-7 compressor module, and the laboratory notes that the measurement chain was recalibrated immediately before the series.

The 42 dB(A) result confirms the positioning of the XK-7 compressor module as the quietest unit in the current Kaltwerk range. Tonal components were assessed and found to be below the audibility threshold defined in the procedure; the dominant spectrum is broadband with a soft tonal peak near 210 hertz at maximum speed only.

Caveats. The chamber result will differ from free-field installations because of ground reflections and wall proximity. The laboratory recommends a 2.5 metre clearance from reflective facades when engineers need field noise performance close to the published 42 dB(A) figure.

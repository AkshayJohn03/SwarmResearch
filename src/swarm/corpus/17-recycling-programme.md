# End-of-Life Recycling Programme

Kaltwerk operates a producer-funded take-back and recycling programme covering every K-Series unit sold in the European Economic Area, regardless of the current owner. The programme meets the WEEE framework obligations and the company's own material-recovery targets.

Process. When a unit reaches end of life, the installing partner schedules a decommissioning visit. The technician recovers the R-290 charge with a spark-free recovery machine, documents the recovered mass against nameplate, and labels the unit for transport. Recovery yield targets are above 95 percent of nameplate charge, and the recovered propane is filtered and re-refined for reuse in industrial refrigeration rather than burned off.

Material routing. After refrigerant recovery, modules are disassembled at a regional partner facility. Copper pipework and the stainless steel plate stack of the heat exchanger module enter closed scrap loops. The compressor is drained and either rebuilt as a service exchange unit or stripped for its copper and steel; rotor magnets from the variable-speed drive feed a recycling pilot that aims to reclaim rare-earth material for new rotor sets. Electronics boards are processed through a certified smelter chain, and the isolation frame returns to the component supplier as a deposit loop.

Reporting. Participating partners receive an annual material account showing collected masses per category and the associated avoided-emissions credit. The programme handbook sets the target that at least 88 percent of unit mass is reused or materially recycled, with the remainder being thermally recovered plastics and process losses. Decommissioning documentation is a required attachment for the final settlement of any decommissioning incentive paid to partners.

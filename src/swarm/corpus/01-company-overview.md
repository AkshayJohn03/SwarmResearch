# Kaltwerk Modular Heating AG: Company Overview

Kaltwerk Modular Heating AG is a German manufacturer of modular air-to-water heat pumps for residential and light-commercial buildings. The company was founded in 2011 in Leipzig and currently employs around 640 people across engineering, manufacturing, and a European service organisation. Its product philosophy is built on serviceable modules rather than monolithic appliances: a cooling module, a hydronic module, and a control module can be replaced independently in the field, which keeps repair visits short and avoids full-unit replacements.

The flagship product is the Kaltwerk K-Series, a modulating heat pump built around the XK-7 compressor module. The XK-7 is a variable-speed rotary compressor developed with a Japanese drive supplier and manufactured in two qualified plants, one in Saxony and one in northern Italy. Company materials describe the XK-7 as the quietest compressor in its class, a claim that marketing repeats frequently and that the acoustic laboratory characterises more carefully in its published test reports.

All K-Series units use R-290 propane as the refrigerant, chosen for its very low global warming potential and wide availability without quota restrictions. The control stack runs KaltOS, an in-house firmware platform with over-the-air updates and an open local API for building management systems.

Kaltwerk sells through a network of certified installation partners rather than directly to homeowners. The partner programme includes mandatory training on propane handling, commissioning checklists, and a shared telemetry portal for warranty diagnostics. In 2024 the company opened a second assembly line at the Leipzig plant to satisfy demand from the German subsidy programmes, and the plant now anchors most of the company's European volume.

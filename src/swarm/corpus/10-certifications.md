# Certifications and Regulatory Compliance

K-Series heat pumps are certified against the European framework for heat pump comfort units. This page summarises the current certificates and what they commit the manufacturer to.

Product safety and performance. Units carry the CE mark with declarations of conformity for the Machinery Directive, the Low Voltage Directive, and the EMC Directive. Efficiency is declared according to EN 14825 for seasonal figures and EN 14511 for rating-point figures, with the certificates issued by an independent notified body in Nuremberg. Acoustic declarations follow EN 12102-1, and the declared sound power values are derived from the laboratory sound pressure measurements of the compressor module and cabinet components.

Refrigerant compliance. Because the units use R-290 propane, the F-gas quota system does not apply to the refrigerant charge, which simplifies servicing across borders. Installers still need the flammable-refrigerant handling certificate under the relevant EN 378 room-area rules, and the installation manual reproduces the required labelling.

Ecodesign. Energy labelling classes are published per package on the European product database, and the company's eprel entries list both the outdoor unit and the common system packages. The Ecodesign lot-1 requirements for minimum seasonal efficiency are met across the range without electrical backup in the reference climates.

Factory audits. The Leipzig plant holds ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certificates, renewed in 2024 after a two-stage audit. The notified body performs an annual production surveillance visit covering the compressor sub-assembly line, including a random acoustic re-test of shipped modules against the declared figures. Certificate copies and declaration documents are downloadable from the partner portal without registration.

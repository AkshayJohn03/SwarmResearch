# Preventive Maintenance Schedule

The modular architecture keeps preventive maintenance short and predictable. This schedule lists every task, its interval, and which role may perform it. Intervals assume normal duty in a residential application; hard-water regions and coastal sites follow the appendix derating table.

Owner tasks. Once a year: visual check of the outdoor unit for airflow obstruction, snow or leaf build-up, and legible refrigerant labelling; confirm condensate drainage; check system pressure on the display and top up treated water if it dropped below the marked band.

Partner tasks, every two years: visual leak inspection of refrigerant joints with an electronic detector, water-quality sampling against the hardness table, cleaning of the flow filter, and a readout of the per-module health ledger including inverter temperature histograms. Partner tasks, every six years: full performance verification of the heat exchanger module against its commissioning baseline, torque check of electrical terminals, and replacement of the hydronic module's internal filter cartridge.

Compressor module. The compressor requires no periodic internal service. Its health is tracked through runtime hours, start counts, and the factory acoustic fingerprint compared during every partner visit; deviations beyond the tolerance band trigger a diagnostic visit rather than a scheduled intervention. Fan bearings are the only scheduled mechanical replacement in the outdoor unit, at ten-year intervals.

Record keeping. Every partner visit must be logged in the portal against the unit serial number within 48 hours. The maintenance log is a precondition for transferring warranty to a new owner and for claiming the module substitution right after repeated failures.

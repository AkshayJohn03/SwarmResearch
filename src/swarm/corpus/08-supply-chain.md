# Compressor Supply Chain and Dual Sourcing

Kaltwerk qualified a second compressor manufacturing site in northern Italy in late 2024, completing the dual-sourcing commitment made after the 2023 shortage year. Both sites build the XK-7 compressor module to a shared control plan, and units from either source pass the same inline acoustic screening before shipping to the Leipzig plant.

Supplier management. The drive electronics for the XK-7 compressor module come from a Japanese semiconductor partner with a second-source pin-compatible gate driver qualified on the line. Kaltwerk holds rolling twelve-month forecast agreements and a strategic buffer of two weeks of compressor inventory, a policy introduced after the 2023 shortage described in the historical capacity review.

Transport and customs. Compressor modules arrive by rail from both plants with a 48-hour transit window; the buffer inventory absorbs typical customs or rail delays without schedule impact. Packaging is returnable, and the packaging loop audit reported a loss rate under 2 percent last year.

Risk register. The current top supply risks for the XK-7 compressor module are, in order: a single rare-earth supplier for the rotor magnet set, gate-driver allocation during industry-wide chip demand spikes, and the coating line at the Italian site, which is the only facility qualified for the marine-grade aluminum surface treatment. Mitigations include a qualified alternate coating vendor in qualification and a magnet recycling pilot with the end-of-life programme.

Procurement policy requires that any change to materials or sub-suppliers on the XK-7 compressor module passes a full requalification run including acoustic screening, thermal cycling, and a 1,000-hour endurance test before it may enter series production.
